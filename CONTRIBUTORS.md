# Contributors

Vela is made possible by people who share their time, knowledge, and creativity.

Thank you to everyone who improves the project through code, design, testing,
documentation, reviews, bug reports, and community support.

## Contributors

- **[@Lowell137](https://github.com/Lowell137)** — Development, feature contributions, improvements, and bug fixes.

## Third-party code

- **[Material Color Utilities](https://github.com/material-foundation/material-color-utilities)**

This list is not exhaustive. See the [GitHub contributors graph](https://github.com/vela-desktop/vela/graphs/contributors)
for more people who have contributed code, and the project's issues and pull requests
for the many other ways people help.
